A **certificate** is a proof of completion that CourseLit issues automatically when a learner finishes a course. Certificates are available for courses only.

## Turn on certificates

Open the course's **Settings** tab, find the **Certificates** card and turn on **Issue certificates**. The card reads "Learners get a certificate when they finish every lesson." You can also turn it on from the **Issue certificates** item on the **Overview** checklist.

Turning certificates on is optional and does not affect whether the course can go live.

## Customise the certificate

With certificates on, a **Certificate Template** section appears in the card. Select the arrow to expand it, change any of the fields and select **Save certificate template**.

| Field | What it is | Default if left empty |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Certificate Title | The heading. | Certificate of Completion |
| Certificate Subtitle | The line before the learner's name. | This certificate is awarded to |
| Certificate Description | The line after the learner's name, up to 400 characters. | for completing the course. |
| Signature Name | The person who signs. | The name of the product's creator |
| Signature Designation | Their role, such as Course Instructor. | Empty |

Below the form, pick a **Signature image** and a **Logo** from your media library. Images save as soon as you choose them. Select **Preview** in the card header to see what a certificate looks like with sample learner data.

Each course has its own template, so you can give every course its own wording.

## When a learner gets a certificate

CourseLit issues the certificate when a learner has completed every live lesson in the course. Lessons count as completed when the learner selects **Mark complete**. Quizzes must be passed first, and SCORM lessons must have been used.

A course with no live lessons never issues certificates. Each learner receives one certificate per course. If you add new live lessons later, learners who already have a certificate keep it.

Certificates are only issued if **Issue certificates** is on at the moment the learner completes the course.

## Where learners find it

- On finishing the last lesson, the learner sees a **View certificate** link.
- In their dashboard, the course card shows a **Certificate** label, and the **Certificates** card lists every certificate with its issue date.
- The certificate page is marked **Verified certificate** and shows the logo, the title, the learner's name, the course title, the signature, the date of completion and a certificate ID. The learner can select **Print certificate**.

Each certificate has its own address, so a learner can share it, and anyone with the address can verify it.
