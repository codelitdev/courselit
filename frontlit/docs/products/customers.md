The **Customers** page for a product lists everyone who has, or has asked for, access to it. Use it to see progress, give access to someone directly and end access.

## Open the customer list

Open the product, then open the **More actions** menu (three dots) in the header and choose **Customers**. You can also select the **Customers** tile on the Overview tab. For a course the page reads "View learners and their course progress." For a download it reads "View customers with access to this product."

Use **Search customers…** and select **Search** to find someone by name or email. The list shows 25 people per page.

## What the list shows

| Column | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Name | The customer's name and email. Select it to open their contact record. |
| Status | The state of their access, such as active, pending or expired. |
| Progress (Downloaded for a download) | A progress bar and percentage for a course, with a **View** link. For a download, **Yes** or **Not yet**. |
| Signed up | When they joined. |
| Last active | When they were last active. |
| Subscription | For paid plans: **Active**, **Past due**, **Ended**, or the number of instalments paid such as "2/6 paid". |

Select **View** next to a course progress bar to open "Progress" for that learner. It lists every live lesson with a tick for each one they completed.

## Invite a customer

You can give someone access directly, without a payment. The product must be live.

<Steps>

<Step>

### Open the invite dialog

In the product header, open **More actions** and choose **Invite a customer**. You need permission to manage contacts.

</Step>

<Step>

### Enter their details

Enter the learner's **Name** and **Email**. The email is required.

</Step>

<Step>

### Send the invitation

Select **Invite customer**. CourseLit creates a learner membership for that email address, and emails them with the subject "You have been invited to" followed by the product title. The email has a **Sign in to get started** button that takes them to the product.

</Step>

</Steps>

If the email address does not belong to a learner in your school yet, CourseLit creates the account for you. A person who already has access is not emailed again.

## Cancel a subscription or remove access

Open the actions menu at the end of a customer's row.

| Action | When it is available | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cancel subscription | The customer has an active or past-due subscription. | They are not charged again and keep access until the end of the current billing period. |
| Remove access | The customer's access is not already expired or rejected. | They lose access immediately. If they have a subscription, it is cancelled straight away. They are not refunded automatically. |

Both actions ask you to confirm. Select **Keep** to back out.
