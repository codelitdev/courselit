A **lead magnet** is a free [digital download](/docs/products/digital-downloads) that people get by giving you their email address. Everyone who signs up joins your contacts, so you can email them later.

Lead magnets are only available for digital downloads.

## Create a lead magnet

When you create the download, choose **Lead magnet** under **How people get it**. The option reads "Free for an email address. Grows your mailing list." CourseLit gives the product a free payment plan called **Free** automatically.

To turn an existing download into a lead magnet, open its **Settings** tab and switch on **Lead magnet**. The card reads "Give it away in exchange for an email address. People who sign up get the download and join your contacts."

## Requirements

A lead magnet needs **exactly one** payment plan, and it must be free. This is checked when you turn the switch on.

- If your download has no plan, or more than one plan, or a paid plan, the switch is disabled and the card shows "To turn this on, keep exactly one plan and make it free."
- While **Lead magnet** is on, keep the free plan as the only plan. The card shows "People sign up with their email to get it. It uses the free plan, so keep that as the only plan."

Switch **Lead magnet** off if you want to add other plans.

## What happens when someone signs up

When a person signs up for a live lead magnet with their email address:

- They are added to your contacts as a subscriber.
- They get access to the download.
- They receive an email with a link to get it.

They can then use **Download files** on the download's page. See [Digital downloads](/docs/products/digital-downloads).

## Launch it

Add your files, set the product live and share its link. On a live lead magnet, **Overview** counts **Downloads** and your Customers page lists everyone who signed up. See [Customers](/docs/products/customers).
