Open a product from the **Products** page to manage it. The product page has a header with its name, a status label (**Draft**, **Live** or **Offline**), its link and a summary such as "Course · 3 of 5 lessons live".

## The three tabs

| Tab | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| Overview | Your launch checklist before the product is live, and sales and customer numbers after. |
| Content (Files for a download) | Sections and lessons. See [Sections and lessons](/docs/products/sections-and-lessons). |
| Settings | Details, payment plans, visibility, discussions, certificates and deletion. |

Team members who can only read products see just the Overview with its numbers.

## Overview before you go live

While a product is a draft, Overview shows **Before you go live** with a checklist. Only the first two items are needed to go live.

| Item | Needed to go live | What it checks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Add a payment plan | Yes | At least one active plan. |
| Add your first lesson, or Make at least one lesson live (Add your files for a download) | Yes | At least one live lesson or file. |
| Choose who can find it | No | Whether the product is listed on your site. |
| Write a description | No | Whether the description has text. |
| Add a featured image | No | Whether an image is set. |
| Turn on discussions | No (courses) | Whether discussions are on. |
| Issue certificates | No (courses) | Whether certificates are on. |

Each item has a button that jumps to the setting. The **Ready to go live?** card on the right has the **Make course live** button. See [Publish a product and control visibility](/docs/products/publish-and-visibility).

## Overview after you go live

Once live, Overview shows numbers for a period you choose (**1 day**, **1 week**, **30 days**, **90 days**, **1 year** or **Lifetime**):

- **Sales**, with a chart.
- **Customers**, which links to the [customer list](/docs/products/customers).
- **People who completed the course** for a course, or **Downloads** for a digital download.

If a course still has draft lessons, a notice offers **Review drafts**. The optional checklist items remain under **Make it better** until you finish them.

## Edit details

Open **Settings** and use the **Details** card.

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Name | The title shown on the sales page and at checkout. Up to 200 characters. |
| Link | The part of the address after `/p/`. Changing it breaks links you have already shared. If another product or page uses it, you see "Another product or page already uses this link." |
| Description | A rich text description. It appears on the sales page. |
| Featured image | Select **Choose image** (or **Change image**) to pick a JPEG, PNG, GIF or WebP image from your media library, or upload one. 1200 by 630 pixels works best. |

When you change the name, link or description, an unsaved-changes bar appears with **Discard** and **Save details**. The featured image saves as soon as you pick it.

## Edit the sales page

Every product has a sales page. Select **Edit sales page** in the product header to open it in the full-screen page editor. You need permission to edit the website. The sales page is public only while the product is live.

## Other actions

The **More actions** menu (three dots) in the product header has:

- **Copy link to sales page**.
- **Customers**, which opens the [customer list](/docs/products/customers).
- **Invite a customer**.
- **Take course offline** (or **Take download offline**) while the product is live.

## Delete a product

At the bottom of **Settings**, select **Delete course** (or **Delete download**). Deleting removes the product's content, payment plans and everyone's access, and it cannot be undone. To confirm, type `delete` in the dialog.

You cannot delete a product that has financial history, meaning it has been paid for. You see "This product has financial history and cannot be deleted." In that case, [take it offline](/docs/products/publish-and-visibility) instead.
