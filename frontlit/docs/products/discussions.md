**Discussions** give the learners of a course a space to ask questions and talk to each other while they study. Discussions are available for courses only, and they are off by default.

## Turn on discussions

Open the course's **Settings** tab, find the **Discussions** card and turn on the switch. The card reads "A discussion space for this course." You can also use the **Turn on discussions** item on the **Overview** checklist.

Turning discussions on creates a discussion space named after your course. If you rename the course, the space is renamed too.

## What learners see

Learners who have joined the course see a **Discussions** button in the header of the course viewer, on any lesson page. Selecting it opens a **Discussion space** panel beside the lesson with the course's posts. Learners can create posts, comment, reply and react, the same way they do in your community. See [Communities](/docs/communities).

The button is hidden while discussions are off. The discussion space itself is kept, so turning discussions back on reuses it.

When you [preview the course](/docs/products/preview-a-course), the panel does not show posts. You see "Discussion space content is available to enrolled learners."

## Review reported content

When discussions are on, a **Reports** section appears under the switch in the same card. Learners can report posts, comments and replies, and reported items land here for you to review.

Use the filter to switch between **To review**, **Removed**, **Kept** and **All**. Removed content is hidden from members right away, and the author is not told who reported it. For each item in **To review**, choose one of:

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Keep post (or comment, or reply) | Leaves the content up. You must add a note under **Why it stays up**, which is kept with the report for your team. |
| Remove post (or comment, or reply) | Removes the content. |

You can reopen a handled report with **Review again**. When there is nothing left to review, the list reads "Nothing to review. All clear." Select **Load more reports** to see older reports.

## Tips

- Discussions are separate from your school's community. Your community's own spaces and moderation are covered in [Communities](/docs/communities).
- Turning discussions off hides the **Discussions** button for learners. It does not delete the space.
