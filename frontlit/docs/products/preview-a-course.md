**Preview** lets you walk through a course exactly as a learner would, before it is live and without giving yourself access. Previewing does not create a membership, record progress or change anything about your school.

Preview is available for courses only, and you need permission to manage products.

## Open a preview

<Steps>

<Step>

### Select Preview

Open the course and select **Preview** in the product header.

</Step>

<Step>

### Browse as a learner

The course opens in a new tab on your school's site, in the course viewer. A **Preview** badge in the header tells you that you are in preview mode, and a toast shows when the preview expires.

</Step>

</Steps>

A preview lasts 15 minutes. After that, select **Preview** again to open a fresh one.

If your school has no public address yet, CourseLit tells you "This school has no public address to preview on yet."

## What a preview shows

In preview you can open every lesson in the course. Compared with what a learner sees, a preview adds:

- **Draft courses.** You can preview a course before it is live.
- **Draft lessons.** Lessons that are still in **Draft** appear.
- **Locked content.** Lessons that need a membership, and sections held back by a [drip schedule](/docs/products/drip-schedules), are open.

## What a preview does not do

- You cannot mark lessons complete, and no progress or certificates are recorded.
- **Quiz** lessons do not show their questions. You see "Quiz content is available after enrollment." To test a quiz, [invite yourself](/docs/products/customers) as a learner with a second email address.
- The discussion panel does not show posts. You see "Discussion space content is available to enrolled learners."
- Previews do not change how learners see your course. Learners still see only live lessons in a live course.

<Callout type="warning" title="Keep the preview address private">

The preview address contains a temporary key. Anyone who has it can view the course until it expires, so do not share it publicly.

</Callout>

## Preview a sales page

To see how your public sales page looks, make the course live and open it from **Copy link to sales page** or **View sales page**. You can edit the page with **Edit sales page**. See [Manage a product](/docs/products/manage-a-product).
