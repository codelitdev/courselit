A **drip schedule** holds back a section of a course until a set time. Learners see the section locked until then. Use it to pace a course by the week, or to open a bonus section on a launch date.

Drip is set per section, on the **Content** tab of a course.

## Set a drip schedule

<Steps>

<Step>

### Open the drip settings

In the section header, open the **More** menu (three dots) and choose **Drip release**. A panel opens under the header.

</Step>

<Step>

### Choose when the section unlocks

Under **Release**, choose one of:

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Right away | The section opens as soon as the learner joins. This is the default, and it turns drip off. |
| Days after the previous section | The section opens a number of days after the previous section unlocks. Enter **Days after previous section**, a whole number of 1 or more. |
| On a date | The section opens on a fixed date and time. Pick a **Release date**. |

</Step>

<Step>

### Save

Select **Done**. Sections with a schedule show a badge on their header, such as "Opens 7 days after previous" or "Opens 3 Mar". Select the badge to change it.

</Step>

</Steps>

## How relative schedules add up

With **Days after the previous section**, each delay counts from the time the previous scheduled section unlocks. The first count starts from the day the learner's access begins.

For example, if section 2 opens 7 days after the previous section and section 3 opens 7 days after the previous section, a learner who joins today gets section 2 in 7 days and section 3 in 14 days. A section set to **Right away** does not change that count.

A date-based section opens on its date for every learner, whenever they joined.

## What learners see

Until a section unlocks, its lessons show as locked in the course viewer. If a learner opens a locked lesson, they see "Content Locked" and the date it unlocks. The lock applies to every lesson in the section, including [free preview](/docs/products/free-preview-lessons) lessons.

## Email learners when a section unlocks

Under a scheduled section's drip settings, select **Edit unlock email** to write an email that goes out when the section unlocks. The editor opens full-screen.

- Write a **subject** and the email's content.
- Turn on **Send when the section unlocks** to send it. The email is off until you turn it on, and you need a subject and content before you can turn it on.
- Use **Save** to save. The editor shows **Saving…** and **Saved**.

The email goes to each active learner when the section unlocks for them. Learners whose section unlocked before you turned the email on do not receive it. Unlock emails go out as written, with no marketing footer or merge tags.

## Tips

- Drip only controls when content unlocks. You can still keep individual lessons in **Draft** until they are ready. See [Sections and lessons](/docs/products/sections-and-lessons).
- When you [preview a course](/docs/products/preview-a-course), locked and scheduled sections are shown unlocked so you can review them.
