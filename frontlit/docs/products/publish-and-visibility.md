Every product has two independent controls. **Live status** decides whether anyone can see or join it. **Who can find it** decides whether it is listed on your site or only reachable by link.

## The three statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Draft | The product has never been live. Nobody can see it. |
| Live | The product is published. Its sales page works and people can join. |
| Offline | The product was live once and is now back to draft. |

While a product is not live, a banner at the top of the product page reads "This course is a draft. Nobody can see it until it's live." (or "This course is offline. Nobody can see or join it until it's live again."), with a **Manage publishing** button.

## Make a product live

<Steps>

<Step>

### Meet the two requirements

A product needs both of these before it can go live:

- At least one **active payment plan**. See [Payment plans](/docs/products/payment-plans).
- At least one **live lesson** (or **live file** for a download). See [Sections and lessons](/docs/products/sections-and-lessons).

If you try without a plan, you see "Add a payment plan before going live."

</Step>

<Step>

### Go live

Open the product's **Overview** tab. In **Ready to go live?**, check that plans and content show a tick, then select **Make course live** (or **Make download live**). The button stays disabled until both requirements are met.

</Step>

<Step>

### Share the link

A confirmation card shows the product's link. Select **Copy link** or **View sales page**. If the product is listed on your site, people can join straight away. If it is hidden, share the link with the people you want to join.

</Step>

</Steps>

The other items on the checklist, such as the description, featured image, discussions and certificates, are optional. They help people decide to join.

## Choose who can find it

Open the **Settings** tab and use **Who can find it**. The choice saves as soon as you pick it.

| Option | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Anyone | Listed on your site's products page. |
| Only people with the link | Hidden from your site. Share the link yourself. |

New products start as **Only people with the link**. A product only appears on your site while it is live, whichever option you pick.

The product's link has the form `your-school.courselit.app/p/your-product`. You can change the end of it under **Link** in **Details**, but that breaks links you have already shared.

## Take a product offline

Open the **More actions** menu and choose **Take course offline** (or **Take download offline**), then confirm **Take offline**. The dialog explains what happens:

- Its sales page stops working and nobody new can join.
- People who already joined cannot open it until you go live again.
- Nothing is deleted.

When the product is offline, select **Go live again** in the product header to republish it. The product comes back with the same plans and content.

## Draft and live lessons

A live product still hides its draft lessons from learners. If a live course has draft lessons, Overview shows how many and offers **Review drafts**. See [Sections and lessons](/docs/products/sections-and-lessons).

## Preview before you publish

You can look at a course as a learner would before it is live. See [Preview a course](/docs/products/preview-a-course).
