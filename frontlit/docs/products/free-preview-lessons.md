A **free preview** lesson can be opened by anyone from the course's sales page, without joining. It lets visitors sample your teaching before they buy or sign up.

## Turn on a free preview

<Steps>

<Step>

### Open the lesson

On the **Content** tab, select the lesson's title. Or create a new lesson with **Add lesson**.

</Step>

<Step>

### Switch on Free preview

Turn on **Free preview**. Its description reads "Anyone can open this lesson from the sales page without joining." Then save the lesson.

</Step>

</Steps>

Lessons are not free previews by default. In the lesson list, a free preview lesson shows "Free preview" under its title.

## What visitors see

On the sales page, the **Content** section lists your sections and lessons. Free preview lessons show a **Preview** badge and open when selected. Other lessons are listed but not clickable.

A free preview lesson must still be a **live** lesson in a **live** course. A draft lesson is hidden from visitors even when it has the free preview switch on.

## Limits

- **Quiz** lessons cannot be free previews. The **Free preview** switch is not shown for quizzes.
- Digital downloads do not have free preview lessons.
- A lesson inside a section with a [drip schedule](/docs/products/drip-schedules) stays locked, even when it is a free preview. Visitors cannot open it, and learners can open it once the section unlocks for them.
