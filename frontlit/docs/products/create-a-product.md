You create a product from the **Products** page. A new product is always a draft, so nobody can see it until you [make it live](/docs/products/publish-and-visibility).

## Before you begin

- You need permission to manage products. Without it, **New product** is hidden and the page shows a permission message.
- You do not need payments set up to create a product. They are only needed for paid plans.

## Create a product

<Steps>

<Step>

### Open the New product page

Open **Products** in the sidebar and select **New product**. The Products page lists your existing products as cards. You can narrow the list with **Filter** (**All**, **Course** or **Digital download**), and each card shows its type, whether it is public or hidden, whether it is a draft or published, and its sales and customer counts.

</Step>

<Step>

### Choose what you are making

Under **What are you making?**, pick one:

| Choice | Best for |
| --- | --- |
| Course | Lessons people work through at their own pace, grouped into sections. |
| Digital download | Guides, templates, presets or ebooks people get straight away. |

<Callout type="warning" title="You cannot change this later">

The product type is fixed once you create the product. Pick the one that fits.

</Callout>

</Step>

<Step>

### Choose how people get a download

For a digital download, **How people get it** asks you to pick one option. You can switch it later in **Settings**.

- **Sell it**: people get the files after they pay or join a free plan.
- **Lead magnet**: free for an email address. See [Lead magnets](/docs/products/lead-magnets).

</Step>

<Step>

### Name it

Enter a **Title**, up to 200 characters. Below the field, CourseLit shows the link the product will get, such as `your-school.courselit.app/p/watercolour-basics`. The link is built from the title. If another product or page already uses it, you see "A product with this title already exists." and need to choose a different title.

</Step>

<Step>

### Create the draft

Select **Create draft**. CourseLit opens the product on its **Content** tab (or **Files** tab for a download), so you can add what learners will get first.

</Step>

</Steps>

Communities are not created here. To set up a community, use **Community** in the sidebar. See [Communities](/docs/communities).

## What a new product contains

- A course starts empty. You add its first section yourself.
- A digital download starts with one section, named **First section**, to hold its files.
- Every product gets a sales page on your site. You can edit it later with **Edit sales page**.
- A new product is **Only people with the link**, so it does not appear on your products page until you change **Who can find it** to **Anyone**.
