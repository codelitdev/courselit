Branding is how visitors recognise your school: its title, subtitle, logo and the colours, fonts and shapes of your theme. Branding and the theme apply to every page of your website.

## Set your title, subtitle and logo

<Steps>

<Step>

### Open Branding

Go to **Website** then **Settings**. The **Branding** tab opens first.

</Step>

<Step>

### Fill in the details

Enter a **Title** and, optionally, a **Subtitle**. The title is required. It starts as your school's name and is used in your site header and in page titles.

</Step>

<Step>

### Save

Click **Save**. The button is enabled only when you have changed the title or subtitle. A **Branding saved.** message confirms it.

</Step>

</Steps>

To add a logo, use the **Logo** field below the form. Click **Select image** to upload an image or pick one you already uploaded. The logo is saved as soon as you choose it, and the message **Logo saved.** appears. Once a logo is set, use **Change image** to replace it or **Remove image** to remove it. **Logo removed.** appears after you remove it.

<Callout type="note">

Branding changes are not drafts. They apply to your live website as soon as they are saved, without publishing a page.

</Callout>

## Choose a theme

A theme sets the colours, fonts, spacing, corner radius and shadows of your whole site, in both light and dark mode. You choose it from inside the [page editor](/docs/website/page-editor).

<Steps>

<Step>

### Open the theme panel

In the page editor, click **Edit theme**.

</Step>

<Step>

### Pick a theme

Under **System Themes**, choose one of the ready-made themes: **Classic**, **Editorial**, **Midnight**, **Neo-Brutalism**, **Studio** or **Terminal**. Click **Use this theme** to apply it. The theme in use is marked **This is your site theme**.

</Step>

<Step>

### Check your pages

Preview your pages in light and dark mode and on desktop, tablet and mobile.

</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout type="warning" title="A theme change is live straight away">

Choosing a theme changes the look of your whole published website immediately. You don't need to publish a page for it to take effect.

</Callout>

## Create your own theme

System themes are read-only. When you edit one, the editor saves your changes as a copy under **Custom Themes**. In the theme panel you can:

- Name your theme in the **Theme name** field.
- Change the colour groups, such as primary, secondary, accent, muted, card, popover and destructive colours, along with their foreground colours, and set separate values for light and dark mode.
- Change fonts for headers, subheaders, body text and buttons, including family, size, weight, spacing and text transform.
- Change border radius, border style and shadow styles.

The section **Custom Themes** shows **No custom themes yet** until you make one. Your custom themes belong to your school and are available on every page.

## Permissions

You need the `storefront:write` permission to change branding and themes.
