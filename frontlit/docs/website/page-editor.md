The page editor is a full-screen visual editor where you build a page from [blocks](/docs/website/blocks). You see the page as visitors will, and click any block to change it.

## Open the editor

Click a page's card on the **Pages** screen, or click **Edit sales page** inside a product or community. The editor opens at `/pages/` followed by the page's id and `/edit`. The **Pages** button at the top left takes you back to where you came from.

## The editor top bar

The bar above the page has the page name on the left and the publishing controls on the right.

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Page URL** box | Shows the page address after a `/`. Edit it to change the address. Only on wide screens. |
| Status badge | Shows **Draft**, **Published** or **Published · changes**. |
| Saving message | Shows **Saving…** while a change is saved, then **Draft saved.** |
| **Discard changes** | Throws away your unpublished edits and goes back to the published version. |
| **Publish changes** | Makes your draft the live page. |

## Edit the page

The builder has its own bar above the page preview with these controls:

- **Add block** opens a picker listing every block you can add.
- **Edit theme** opens the theme panel, described in [Branding and themes](/docs/website/branding-and-themes).
- **Edit SEO settings** opens the search and sharing details for this page.
- Preview buttons switch the preview between **Desktop preview**, **Tablet preview** and **Mobile preview**.
- A mode switch previews the page in **Light mode** or **Dark mode**.

Move your pointer over a block in the page to see its tools:

- **Edit settings** opens that block's settings panel, with its content and a section for styling such as background, padding and content width.
- **Move up** and **Move down** reorder the block.
- **Delete** removes the block from the page draft.
- **Add block below** adds a new block right under it.

## Saving is automatic

Every change you make is saved to your draft straight away. You don't need to click a save button. The status shows **Published · changes** on a live page, or **Draft** on a page that has never been published.

## Publish your changes

When the page looks right, click **Publish changes**. The message **Page published.** appears and visitors see the new version. The button stays disabled when there is nothing new to publish.

To undo your unpublished edits, click **Discard changes**. The page goes back to the version visitors currently see, and the message **Unpublished changes discarded.** appears.

<Callout type="warning" title="The header and footer are shared">

The header and footer are the same on every page of your site. When you change them on one page, the change is part of that page's draft, and it goes live site-wide when you publish any page. **Discard changes** also reverts the shared header and footer to their published versions.

</Callout>

## Change the page address

Type a new address in the **Page URL** box and click outside the box. The editor removes any leading or trailing slashes and saves the new address immediately. If another page already uses it, the change is refused and the old address stays.

The box is disabled for the Homepage, Terms of Service and Privacy policy pages, and for sales pages, because their addresses are fixed.

## Set SEO details

Click **Edit SEO settings** to control how the page appears in search results and when shared. You can set:

- The **Page title**.
- A short summary of the page, used as its description.
- A **Social card image**, shown when the page is shared on social networks.
- **Search engine visibility**, which controls whether search engines may list the page.

Like all other edits, these are saved to your draft and go live when you publish.

## Upload images in the editor

Blocks that show images or videos use your school's [media library](/docs/website/media-library). Choose **Upload or choose image** in a block's settings to upload a file or pick one you uploaded earlier.

## Changes you make with a theme go live immediately

Your theme is not part of the draft. When you pick a theme in the editor, it is applied to your whole website right away, without publishing. Read [Branding and themes](/docs/website/branding-and-themes) before you switch.
