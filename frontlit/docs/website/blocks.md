A page is a stack of blocks. Each block is a ready-made section with its own settings. Click **Add block** in the [page editor](/docs/website/page-editor) to see the picker, choose a block, then click it on the page and use **Edit settings** to fill it in.

## Blocks you can add

| Block | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| Hero | A large introduction with a title, copy and calls to action. |
| Content | A rich-text section with an optional image, for things like an about-us text or a feature explainer. |
| Rich text | Formatted text with paragraphs, headings, lists and links, and no title or media. |
| Feature grid | A grid of highlights, each with an icon, title and description. |
| Grid | A header with a call to action, plus a responsive grid of cards that each have an icon or image, a title, a description and a link. |
| FAQs | Frequently asked questions shown as an accordion. |
| Pricing | A grid of pricing tiers with features and calls to action. |
| Banner | A strong call-to-action strip. |
| Media | A single image or video, from YouTube, Vimeo or an upload, with an optional click-to-play preview. |
| Marquee | A horizontally scrolling row of text or icons, useful for logo clouds or highlight tickers. |
| Code snippet | A command or short piece of code with a copy button, like an install command. |
| Embed | A web address shown in an iframe, or a script or iframe snippet pasted from another tool. |
| Newsletter signup | A signup form that adds visitors to your contacts as subscribers. |
| Home hero | Your school name, tagline and first offer. Hidden until you have them. |
| Offers | Your published courses, downloads and community. Hidden when there are none. |

<Callout type="note" title="Pricing blocks are for display only">

The **Pricing** block is a design element you fill in by hand. It doesn't read your products or take payments. To sell something, give your product or community a [payment plan](/docs/products) and send visitors to its sales page.

</Callout>

## Newsletter signup

The **Newsletter signup** block collects an email address. Each visitor who signs up becomes a contact in your school and is subscribed to your marketing emails. You can then email them from [Marketing](/docs/marketing). Its settings are:

| Setting | What it controls |
| --- | --- |
| Title | The heading above the form. Starts as "Sign up for my newsletter". |
| Subtitle | The line under the heading. |
| Button text | The label of the submit button. Starts as "Subscribe". |
| Alignment | Whether the block is aligned left, centre or right. |
| Success message | Shown after a successful signup. Starts as "Thanks for subscribing." |
| Failure message | Shown if the signup fails. Starts as "That didn't work. Please try again." |

## Blocks used on sales pages

Sales pages for your products and communities include extra blocks that read live data from the product or community:

| Block | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Product | The product's details, its payment plans and the checkout button. |
| Community | The community's details and its payment plans, with a join or buy button. |
| Curriculum | The sections and lessons of a course. |

You edit sales pages from **Edit sales page** on the product or community. See [Products](/docs/products) and [Communities](/docs/communities).

## Header and footer

Every page starts with a **Header** and ends with a **Footer**. They are shared by all pages, so you edit them once for the whole site. You can't move or delete them.

- A new school's header shows your logo and site title, links to **Products** and **Blog**, and a **Join** button.
- A new school's footer shows your site name, tagline and copyright, a **Resources** column with a link to the blog, and a **Legal** column linking to **Terms of use** and **Privacy policy**.

Changes to the header and footer are part of the draft of the page you are editing and go live when you publish a page. See [Edit a page](/docs/website/page-editor#publish-your-changes).

## Section styling

Most blocks have a styling section in their settings where you can change the background, vertical padding and content width of that one block. Backgrounds can be a solid colour, an image you upload or one of the built-in patterns and textures.

<Callout type="tip" title="Let an AI assistant do the first draft">

If you connect an AI assistant to your school, it can create and edit pages for you using the same blocks. See [AI assistants](/docs/settings/ai-assistants).

</Callout>
