A new community is offline. Learners cannot see or join it until you make it live. Going live takes a name, at least one plan and, if any plan is paid, a connected payment provider.

You need the `communities:write` permission. Without it, the screen says to ask a school admin to set up the community.

## Start the setup

1. Click **Community** in the left-hand navigation. A new community shows **Start your community** with three steps: **Choose how people join** (required), **Make it yours** and **Invite your learners**.
2. Click **Set up community**. This opens **Set up your community**.

If you have already added a plan but not gone live, the community screen opens with its tabs and a **Go live** button instead. The button leads to the same screen.

## Fill in the setup

<Steps>

<Step>

### Name your community

Enter a **Community name** (up to 200 characters). It is shown on your sales page and at the top of the community. Below the field, you see the address of your sales page.

</Step>

<Step>

### Add at least one plan

In **Plans**, click **Add a plan** and pick free, one time, subscription or EMI. The first plan you add is starred and is shown first on the sales page. See [Community plans](/docs/communities/plans) for every option.

</Step>

<Step>

### Decide who gets in

In **Who gets in**, choose one of:

- **Everyone who joins**: people are in as soon as they sign up or pay.
- **Only people I approve**: people ask to join and tell you why. You approve or reject them from the **Members** tab. When you choose this, a **Question to ask** field appears with the suggestion "Why do you want to join this community?". Enter your own question, or leave it empty to use the default wording.

You can change this later. See [Manage members](/docs/communities/manage-members).

</Step>

<Step>

### Check the checklist and go live

The **Ready to go live?** card shows what is still missing:

- The community has a name.
- There is at least one plan and one of them is starred.
- For paid plans, payments are connected. If they are not, a **Connect payments** link takes you to the payment settings. See [Settings](/docs/settings).

When every item is checked, click **Make community live**. A notice says "Your community is live." and you return to the community screen.

</Step>

</Steps>

On the right of the setup screen a preview shows what your sales page will look like with your plans.

## After going live

- Click **View community** to open the community as a member sees it.
- Open the **More actions** menu (three dots) and choose **Copy sales page link** to copy the address to share. The sales page is at your school's `/join` address.
- Fill in the picture, description and welcome banner. See [Community details](/docs/communities/details).
- To pause the community later, see [Take the community offline](/docs/communities/take-offline).
