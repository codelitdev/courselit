The **Details** tab is the first tab of the community screen. Each card saves on its own, and a card shows its own save buttons once you edit text in it. If you leave the page with unsaved changes, you are warned first.

You need the `communities:write` permission.

## Name, image and description

| Field | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Community name | Shown on your sales page and at the top of the community. Required, up to 200 characters. |
| Featured image | Click **Add image** to choose a JPEG, PNG, GIF or WebP file, **Replace** to change it, or remove it. Saved immediately. |
| Description | A rich-text description of what members get from joining. It is shown on the sales page. |

Click **Save details** to save the name and description, or **Discard** to go back to what was saved.

## Edit the sales page

The name, image and description headline your community's sales page, which is a page on your website at `/join`. Click **Edit sales page** to open the page editor (you need the `storefront:write` permission), where you can add testimonials, FAQs, a video or any other sections around the community block. See [Website](/docs/website).

## Welcome banner

The **Welcome banner** card has a switch, **Show welcome banner**. Turn it on to pin a message to the top of Home, where members land after joining. Write the **Message** and click **Save message**.

Change the message for launches, events or breaks. Members always see the newest version. Turning the switch off removes the banner from Home.

## Who gets in

This card holds the same choice as at setup: **Everyone who joins** or **Only people I approve**, plus the **Question to ask** that applies to approval. The switch between the two saves immediately. A change of the question needs **Save question**.

## Plans

The **Plans** card lists your plans. See [Community plans](/docs/communities/plans).

## Preview

The right-hand side shows a preview of Home, the page members land on after joining. Use the desktop and mobile buttons to switch between **Desktop preview** and **Mobile preview**, and **Open sales page** to open your live sales page in a new tab. The preview updates as you type, before you save.
