Members can report any post, comment or reply written by someone else. Reports collect on the **Reports** tab of the community screen, where you decide whether the content stays or goes.

A number on the tab shows how many reports are waiting for review. You need the `communities:moderate` permission to see the tab.

## Review a report

The tab lists reports with a filter above them:

| Filter | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| To review | Reports nobody has handled yet. This is the default. |
| Removed | Reports where you removed the content. |
| Kept | Reports where you decided the content stays. |
| All | Everything. |

Each report shows:

- The type of content: **Post**, **Comment** or **Reply**, and how long ago it was reported.
- The reason the member gave, after **Reported for**.
- The author and the reported content, including its images, videos, attachments and GIFs.

If the content has been deleted since, the report says that it is no longer available.

## Keep or remove the content

For a report in **To review**, choose one of:

- **Remove post**, **Remove comment** or **Remove reply**: the content is hidden from members right away. The report moves to **Removed**.
- **Keep post**, **Keep comment** or **Keep reply**: the content stays. A dialog titled **Keep this post?** asks you to note why it stays up. Enter the note and click **Keep it**. The note is stored with the report for your team, and the report moves to **Kept**.

The author is not told who reported their content.

If you change your mind, click **Review again** on a handled report to send it back to **To review**.

## Moderators in the community

Members with the **Moderator** role can act on content directly in the community, without opening the admin: they can pin posts to the top of a feed and delete other members' posts and comments. See [Manage members](/docs/communities/manage-members) to give someone this role.

## Reports about course discussions

Reports about the discussion of a course do not appear here. They are on the course's own settings, under **Discussions**, in a **Reports** list that works the same way. See [Products](/docs/products).

## What members see when they report

A member opens the actions menu (three dots) on someone else's post, comment or reply and chooses **Report**. A **Report content** dialog asks them to describe why the content should be reviewed. Members cannot report their own content.
