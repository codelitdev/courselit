If you need to pause your community, you can take it offline. Nothing is deleted, and you can make it live again whenever you want.

You need the `communities:write` permission.

## Take it offline

1. Open **Community**.
2. Click the **More actions** button (three dots) at the top and choose **Take offline**.
3. In the **Take the community offline?** dialog, click **Take offline**.

A notice says "Your community is offline. Learners can't see or join it." The community title then shows an **Offline** badge, and the **View community** button is replaced by **Go live**.

## What happens

- Learners cannot see or join the community.
- Members lose access until you bring it back.
- Posts, spaces, plans, members and settings are kept as they are.
- You cannot invite people to an offline community.

Taking the community offline does not cancel the subscriptions of paying members. Cancel or refund them with your payment provider if you want to stop billing.

## Bring it back

Click **Go live** and then **Make community live**, as described in [Set up and go live](/docs/communities/set-up). The community must still have an active, starred plan.

## Deleting a community

The admin has no option to delete the community. Every school has exactly one community, created with the school, so the way to take it out of view is to take it offline.
