This page describes the community from the member's side.

## Finding and joining

Learners reach your community through its sales page at your school's `/join` address. It shows the featured image, the name, the description and the number of members. When you offer several plans, a button for each plan lets them choose one, and the price of the selected plan is shown next to **Join community**.

**Join community** starts checkout. Learners sign in if they have not done so, and:

- On a free plan that lets everyone in, they are members right away.
- On a free plan that needs your approval, they answer your question and click **Request to join**, and then wait. They see "Your request to join" followed by the community name "is waiting for approval. We'll let you know." on their dashboard until you decide.
- On a paid plan they pay and become members once the payment is confirmed.

See [Checkout and purchases](/docs/learners/purchases) for the checkout page. Invited people skip all of this and join immediately, see [Manage members](/docs/communities/manage-members).

## The feed

Members use the same dashboard as learners, see [The learner dashboard](/docs/learners/learner-dashboard):

- **Home** shows the community's name, your welcome banner and the latest posts from every space they can open.
- Each space has its own page from the **Spaces** group in the sidebar.
- Pinned posts are marked and stay at the top.

## Posting

Members who can post in a space see a **Write something…** box. It opens a dialog where they pick the space, add a title, write the post and optionally attach files, add an image or video, or insert a GIF. They click **Post** to publish. Posts use a rich text editor.

Spaces where **Who can post** is set to **Admin** show posts but no composer to members.

## Reacting and replying

- Members react to posts, comments and replies with six emoji: thumbs up, heart, smile, party, sad and surprised. A reaction can be taken back by clicking it again.
- They open a post to read its comments and replies, and to write their own.
- Authors can edit and delete their own posts and comments from the actions menu (three dots).
- Members can turn notifications for a post on or off.

## Notifications

When someone posts, comments, replies or reacts, members are notified in the app and by email, according to the choices on their **Notifications** page. See [Learner account](/docs/learners/account).

## Leaving

On the Home page, the community's name has a menu with **Leave community**. Leaving ends access right away to the community, its spaces and any products that were included with the membership. If the member pays by subscription or installments, the billing is cancelled at the same time and the dialog says so: "Access ends now, not at the end of your current billing period." The button then reads **Leave and cancel billing**.

Team members of your school stay in the community and do not see the leave option.

## When the community is offline

When you take the community offline, learners cannot see or join it. See [Take the community offline](/docs/communities/take-offline).
