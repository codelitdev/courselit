---
title: Grant access to additional products
description: Include courses and digital downloads with a community plan so that members get them automatically when they join.

You can bundle products into a community plan. Everyone who joins on that plan gets access to the included courses and downloads, with no extra checkout. This works as a welcome bonus, or as the main reason to subscribe.

You need the `communities:write` permission. Only published products can be added.

## Include products in a plan

1. Open **Community** and go to the **Details** tab.
2. In the **Plans** card, click **Add a plan** to create a plan, or click an existing plan to edit it.
3. In the **Included Products** section of the dialog, open **Add Product** and choose a product. Courses and downloads are listed with their type.
4. Repeat for every product you want to include. Each one appears under **Selected Products**. A product that has since been unpublished carries an **Unpublished** label. Click the remove button on a product to take it out again.
5. Click **Save**.

Plans with included products show a badge such as **+2 products** in the plan list, and learners see the included products under the plan at checkout.

## What members get

- When a member's community membership becomes active, they get a separate membership for each included product. For a free plan, this is when they join, or when you approve their request. For a paid plan, it is when the payment is confirmed. Invited members get the products right away.
- The member finds the products in **Products** on their dashboard, like any other product they own.
- The product's **Customers** screen lists them as customers.

## When access ends

Included products follow the community membership:

- If you remove the member or reject their membership, or they leave the community, the included memberships expire with it.
- Archiving a plan does not affect members who are already on it.

If a learner also bought the same product directly, the direct purchase is a separate membership and stays in place.
