You can bundle products into a community plan. Everyone who joins on that plan gets access to the included courses and downloads, with no extra checkout. This works as a welcome bonus, or as the main reason to subscribe.

A plan can include either **All products** of your school, or only the **Selected products** you choose.

You need the `communities:write` permission.

## Include products in a plan

1. Open **Community** and go to the **Details** tab.
2. In the **Plans** card, click **Add a plan** to create a plan, or click an existing plan to edit it.
3. In the **Included Products** section of the dialog, choose one of:
   - **All products**: every published product, including ones you publish later. New products reach the plan's members a few moments after you publish them.
   - **Selected products**: open the **Products** dropdown and tick the products to include. Only published products are listed, with their type. A selected product that has since been unpublished carries an **Unpublished** label so you can untick it. With nothing ticked, the plan includes no products.
4. Click **Save**.

In the plan list, a plan shows **All products**, or how many products it includes, such as **2 products**. At checkout, learners see the included products under the plan, or **Every product included** for an all-products plan.

The product's sales page also offers the community plans that include it, with a line such as "Also included in Makers with the Monthly plan." When every plan of your community includes the product, it reads "Also included with a Makers membership."

## What members get

- When a member's community membership becomes active, they get a separate membership for each included product. For a free plan, this is when they join, or when you approve their request. For a paid plan, it is when the payment is confirmed. Invited members get the products right away.
- The member finds the products in **Products** on their dashboard, like any other product they own.
- The product's **Customers** screen lists them as customers.
- Email sequences that start when a product is purchased start for products a member gets on joining. They don't start for products a member gets later because you published a product or changed the plan.

## Change what a plan includes

Changing a plan's included products also updates its current members, a few moments after you save:

- Products the plan now includes are added to their memberships. This covers switching to **All products** and ticking more products.
- Products the plan no longer includes are taken away. This covers switching from **All products** to **Selected products** and unticking a product. The learner's progress is kept, so it comes back if you include the product again.

A product a member bought directly is never taken away.

## When access ends

Included products follow the community membership:

- If you remove the member or reject their membership, they leave the community, or their payment lapses, the included memberships expire with it.
- If they join again, they get the plan's products back, with their earlier progress.
- Archiving a plan does not affect members who are already on it.

If a learner also bought the same product directly, the direct purchase is a separate membership and stays in place.

## Publishing a product without its own plan

A product needs an active payment plan of its own before you can publish it, unless an active community plan includes it: a plan that includes all products, or one that lists it.
