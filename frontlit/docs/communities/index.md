A community is a place on your school's website where learners ask questions, share their work and talk to each other. Members join through a plan that you set up: free or paid, with or without your approval.

Every school has exactly one community. It is created together with your school, so there is nothing to create. It starts offline, named after your school, with one space called **General**, until you set it up and make it live.

Open it from **Community** in the left-hand navigation. You need the `communities:read` permission to see the item. Most tasks need `communities:write`, and handling reports needs `communities:moderate`.

The community screen has four tabs:

| Tab | What you do there |
| --- | --- |
| Details | Set the name, picture, description, welcome banner, who gets in and the plans. |
| Members | Approve requests, invite people, change roles and remove members. |
| Spaces | Organise discussions into spaces and decide who can see and post in each. |
| Reports | Review content that members reported. |
