A space is a discussion area inside your community, like a channel. Posts always belong to a space, and members see the spaces they can access in the sidebar of their dashboard, under **Spaces**. Their **Home** page combines the posts from all of them.

Your community starts with one space called **General**. Open the **Spaces** tab of the community screen to add more or change how each one works. You need the `communities:write` permission.

## The space list

Each space shows:

- Its name.
- Who can post: **Members post** or **Admins post**.
- **Auto-follow** when the space is followed automatically.
- Who has access: **All community members**, a number of community plans, a product's members or "Staff only" when no rule is set.

## Create a space

1. Click **New space**.
2. Fill in the **New space** dialog:

   | Field | What it does |
   | --- | --- |
   | Name | Required. Shown in the sidebar and on posts. |
   | Description | A short line about the space. |
   | Logo (Lucide icon name) | The name of an icon from the Lucide library, for example `MessagesSquare`. |
   | Who can post | **Members** lets every member who can access the space post. **Admin** limits posting to admins. |
   | Auto-follow on join | When on, members automatically follow the space when they get access to it. |
   | Access | **All community members**, or **Selected plans or products**. |

3. Click **Save**.

## Control who can access a space

Set **Access** to **Selected plans or products** to open a space only to part of your audience. Two kinds of rule are available. You pick at least one.

### Community payment plans

Members on any of the plans you tick get access. Use **Select all** or **Deselect all** to tick every plan at once. Plans you archived are shown with an **Archived** label.

### Product access

Tick a product, and then choose what counts:

- **Any product membership** includes direct purchases and access bundled with another plan.
- **Selected direct-purchase plans** limits access to buyers of specific plans of that product.

A learner gets access when any selected community plan or any product rule matches their active membership. This is the way to create a space for the students of one course, or a members-only space for a premium plan.

<Callout type="note" title="Staff only">

A space with no access rule can only be seen by staff.

</Callout>

## Edit a space

Click **Edit** on a space to change any of its settings.

## Delete a space

Click **Delete** on a space. The **Delete** button is disabled while the community has only one space, so you always keep at least one. In the confirmation dialog choose a space under **Move existing posts to**, then click **Delete space**. Posts, with their comments, reactions, reports and media, are kept under the space you chose. The space itself, its followers and its access rules are deleted permanently.

## Course discussions

Courses with discussions switched on have their own discussion space. It is managed by the course, and it does not appear on this tab. See [Taking a course](/docs/learners/learning-experience).
