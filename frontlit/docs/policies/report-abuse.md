Every page of a school hosted on courselit.app has a **Report this site** link at the bottom. It opens this page.

## How to report

Email **abuse@courselit.app** with:

- the address of the page, for example `https://example-school.courselit.app/some-page`;
- what's wrong with it: phishing, malware, spam, copyright infringement or something else from the [acceptable use policy](/docs/policies/acceptable-use-policy);
- for spam, the email you received, with its headers if you can.

We read every report. For phishing and malware we act as fast as we can, usually by suspending the school. We may not reply to every report, but we follow up when we need more information.

## Copyright

To report a school that uses your work without permission, include the address of the page, a description of your work and where it's published, and a statement that you own it or are authorised to act for the owner.
