This policy applies to every school hosted on courselit.app, on every plan and during the free trial. It covers the school's website, courses, communities, emails, uploaded files and any code added to its pages. Self-hosted CourseLit installs aren't covered.

## What you can't do

Don't use CourseLit to:

- **Deceive people.** No phishing, no pages that imitate another company, bank or service, and no pages that ask for passwords or payment details under false pretences.
- **Spread malware.** No files or code meant to harm visitors' devices, steal data or mine cryptocurrency.
- **Send spam.** Only email people who agreed to hear from you, and honour unsubscribes. Don't buy or rent email lists.
- **Sell what you can't legally sell,** or sell anything you don't deliver.
- **Infringe others' rights.** Only upload courses, videos, images and text that you own or have permission to use.
- **Harm or exploit people.** No content that sexualises minors, promotes violence against people, or harasses others.
- **Abuse the service.** Don't use your school as free file hosting, test the platform's security without permission, or try to get around plan limits.

## What we do when a school breaks this policy

We may remove content, pause email sending, or suspend the school. A suspended school's site, sign-in and email stop straight away. We usually contact the school's owner first, but we act without notice when people are at risk, for example during phishing.

Learners' payments go through the school's own payment account, so refunds and chargebacks are between the school and its payment provider.

## Report a school

If you find a school breaking this policy, [report it](/docs/policies/report-abuse).
