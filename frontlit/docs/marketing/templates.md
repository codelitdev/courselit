A template is a saved email layout. Start a broadcast or a sequence email from a template so every message shares the same look. Open **Marketing** then **Templates** to see yours. Each template is a card with its name, its default subject and a live preview.

## Built-in templates

When you choose a starting point for a new email or template, the chooser offers these built-in templates alongside your own:

| Template | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| Blank | A clean slate for your message. |
| Announcement | Sharing news, launches or school updates. |
| Newsletter | Curated stories, updates and featured content. |
| New user welcome | Welcoming new learners and orienting them to your school. |
| Upsell products | Promoting your courses, memberships and downloads. |

## Create a template

<Steps>

<Step>

### Click New template

On the **Templates** screen, click **New template**.

</Step>

<Step>

### Name it and pick a starting point

Enter a **Template name**, for example `Weekly Digest`, and an optional **Default subject**. Under **Choose a template**, select a built-in template or one of your own to copy. The selection is shown next to the heading.

</Step>

<Step>

### Click Create template

The template is saved. Click its card to open the template editor.

</Step>

</Steps>

You need the `contacts:write` permission to create, edit, duplicate and delete templates.

## Edit a template

Click a template's card to open the editor. The top bar has a **Template name** box, a status that shows **Saved**, **Saving…** or **Unsaved changes**, and a **Save** button. Below it is the [email editor](/docs/marketing/email-editor). If you leave with unsaved edits, you are asked whether to keep editing, discard or save and leave.

Changing a template doesn't change emails you already created from it. Each broadcast or sequence email keeps its own copy of the content.

## Duplicate or delete a template

Use the two icon buttons at the bottom of a template's card. The first duplicates the template so you can adapt a copy. The second opens the **Delete** confirmation, which permanently deletes the template. Deleting can't be undone.
