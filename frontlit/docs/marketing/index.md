Everyone who signs up to your school, buys from you or joins your newsletter becomes a **contact**. You can organise contacts with tags and segments, and email them from the **Marketing** menu.

## What you can do

- Keep a list of **contacts** and find groups of them with filters and saved segments.
- Label contacts with **tags**.
- Send **broadcasts**, one-time emails that go out right away or at a time you choose.
- Build **sequences**, emails that go out automatically when something happens, such as a tag being added or someone getting access to a product.
- Save your favourite layouts as **templates**.
- Write every email in the same visual **email editor**.

## Before you send your first email

Marketing emails need a postal address in their footer. Add your mailing address in [Marketing settings](/docs/marketing/marketing-settings) first, otherwise you can't send a broadcast or activate a sequence.

## Who can use Marketing

The Contacts and Marketing menus use the contacts permissions:

| You want to | You need |
| --- | --- |
| See contacts, broadcasts, sequences and templates | `contacts:read` |
| Edit contacts, create and edit emails, and send or schedule them | `contacts:write` |
| Remove a contact | `contacts:delete` |

See [Permissions](/docs/settings/permissions) for how permissions work.
