A certificate is a proof of completion for a course. You set the certificate up on the course, see [Products](/docs/products). This page explains what happens on the learner's side.

## When a certificate is issued

A learner earns a certificate when both of these are true:

- The course has the certificate switched on.
- The learner has completed every published lesson in the course.

The certificate is issued automatically at that moment, once per learner and course. Downloads do not issue certificates.

## Where learners find it

- Right after completing the last lesson, a **View certificate** link appears beside the **Completed** button.
- On **Products** in the dashboard, the course card shows a **Certificate** mark, and a **Certificates** panel lists every certificate the learner has earned, with the issue date.

Both open the certificate page.

## The certificate page

The certificate page shows:

- Your school's logo when one is set in the certificate design.
- The text **Verified certificate**, the certificate's title and subtitle, the learner's name and the course title.
- The signature, with the signer's name and designation if you set them.
- **Date of Completion** and the certificate ID.

A **Print certificate** button lets the learner print the page or save it as a PDF from the browser's print dialog.

## Verification by anyone

The certificate page does not need a sign-in. Its address contains a verification ID, so a learner can share the link, for example in a CV or on LinkedIn, and anyone who opens it sees that your school issued it. If the ID is not valid, the page says "That certificate could not be verified."
