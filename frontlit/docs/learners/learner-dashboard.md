After signing in, learners land on their dashboard at `/dashboard` on your school's website. The dashboard uses your school's logo, name and theme, so it looks like a part of your website.

## Navigation

The sidebar has these entries:

| Entry | What it opens |
| --- | --- |
| Home | The feed of posts from every community space the learner can access, with your welcome banner on top. |
| Products | **Your products**: every course and download the learner has access to. |
| Spaces | One entry for each community space the learner can open. This group appears only when the learner has access to at least one space. |

At the bottom of the sidebar, the learner's name opens a menu with:

- **Account**: profile and photo. See [Learner account](/docs/learners/account).
- **Notifications**: which notifications the learner receives, on which channel.
- **Purchases**: order history. See [Checkout and purchases](/docs/learners/purchases).
- **Log out**

A bell icon in the page header opens the learner's recent notifications. When there are none it says "No new notifications".

## Your products

The **Products** page shows one card for each course or digital download the learner has access to. A card has:

- The featured image, title and a **Course** or **Digital download** badge.
- A short excerpt of the description.
- For courses, a progress bar with the text "3 of 10 lessons completed" and the percentage.
- A **Certificate** mark when the learner has earned one.
- For paid recurring plans, the subscription state and, where allowed, a **Cancel subscription** button. See [Checkout and purchases](/docs/learners/purchases).

Clicking a card opens the course, see [Taking a course](/docs/learners/learning-experience), or the download, see [Digital downloads](/docs/learners/downloads).

When a learner has nothing yet, the page says "No enrolled products yet" and links to **Browse products**.

Below the cards, a **Certificates** panel lists the learner's certificates, each linking to its verification page. Until they earn one, it says "Complete a course to earn a certificate." See [Certificates](/docs/learners/certificates).

## Home and the community feed

The **Home** page is the community feed. If your school has a community, it shows the community's name at the top with a menu where members can leave the community, a box to **Write something…** when the learner can post, your welcome banner, and the latest posts. See [What members see](/docs/communities/member-experience).

Learners who are not in the community see "You do not have access to any spaces yet." with a **Browse spaces** link to your community's join page.
