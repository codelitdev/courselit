A digital download is a product made of files rather than lessons. Learners with an active membership can get the files from your school's website at any time.

## How a learner downloads

1. The learner signs in and opens the product from **Products** on their [dashboard](/docs/learners/learner-dashboard), or from the link in the invite email.
2. On the product page, they see "You already have access to this download." and a **Download files** button.
3. They click **Download files**. The button changes to **Preparing download…** while a download link is made, then the download starts.

The files of the product are delivered together as one download. A product without any published files has nothing to deliver, so add its files before you invite learners.

<Callout type="info" title="Download links are short-lived">

Every click on **Download files** creates a fresh link for that learner only. A link works once and expires after two days. If a link has been used or has expired, the learner clicks **Download files** again to get a new one.

</Callout>

Without an active membership, nobody can download the files. A learner whose access was removed or expired, or whose payment failed, cannot download.

## See who has downloaded

Open the product's **Customers** screen. For downloads, the **Downloaded** column says **Yes** once the learner has used a download link and **Not yet** until then. See [Product customers](/docs/learners/product-customers).
