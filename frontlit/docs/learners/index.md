A learner is anyone who signs in to your school's website to learn, download or take part in your community. Each school keeps its own learners: signing in to one school gives a person no access to any other school.

Access is always tied to a specific product or to your community. This relationship is called a membership. A learner can have one membership for each product they were given or bought, plus one for your community, and each membership has its own status.
