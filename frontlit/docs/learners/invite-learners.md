You can give anyone access to a product by inviting them by email. They get access straight away, no payment is taken, and they receive an email with a link to sign in.

Before you start:

- The product must be live. You cannot invite someone to a draft or an offline product.
- You need the `contacts:write` permission. Without it, the invite option is not shown.

## Invite a customer

<Steps>

<Step>

### Open the product

Go to **Products** and open the course or download you want to share.

</Step>

<Step>

### Choose Invite a customer

Click the **More actions** button (the three dots) in the product header, then choose **Invite a customer**.

</Step>

<Step>

### Enter their details

Fill in the dialog:

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Name | Optional. If you leave it empty, the learner is named "Learner" until they enter their own name. |
| Email | Required. The learner signs in with this address. |

</Step>

<Step>

### Send the invite

Click **Invite customer**. A confirmation says that the email address now has access to the course or download.

</Step>

</Steps>

## What happens next

- If the person has never been in your school, an account is created for them. If they already have one, the new access is added to it.
- Their membership for the product is **Active** immediately. See [Membership statuses](/docs/learners/membership-status).
- They get an email titled "You have been invited to" followed by the product's title, with a **Sign in to get started** button. The email is only sent when they did not already have access, so inviting the same person twice does not send a second email.
- The learner signs in with a code sent to their email address. See [Learner sign-in](/docs/learners/sign-in).
- The person appears in the product's [Customers](/docs/learners/product-customers) screen and on the **Contacts** screen. On **Contacts** they show as **Newsletter only** until they sign in for the first time.

## Other ways learners get access

| How | Where to read more |
| --- | --- |
| They buy the product or pick its free plan at checkout. | [Checkout and purchases](/docs/learners/purchases) |
| They join your community on a plan that includes the product. | [Grant access to additional products](/docs/communities/included-products) |

To take access away again, see [Product customers](/docs/learners/product-customers).
