Learners manage their own details from **Account** in the menu at the bottom of the dashboard sidebar. The **Account** area has three tabs: **Profile**, **Notifications** and **Purchases**. For purchases, see [Checkout and purchases](/docs/learners/purchases).

## Profile

The **Profile** tab has these parts:

| Part | What the learner can do |
| --- | --- |
| Your profile photo | Choose **Change photo** to upload a JPG, PNG or WebP image up to 2 MB, or **Remove photo**. The photo is visible to other learners, for example next to community posts. |
| Email | Shown for reference, and cannot be changed. |
| Name | The name shown to other learners and in your admin screens. |
| Bio | A short text about themselves, up to 2,000 characters. |
| Email preferences | A switch called **Newsletter and marketing emails**. Sign-in codes and receipts arrive either way. |

Name and bio are saved with **Save changes**. The photo and the email switch save as soon as they are changed. A learner must have a name to save the profile or change their photo.

The same two settings, the name and the marketing switch, are visible to you on the learner's contact page as **Name** and **Mailing subscription**. See [Contacts and learners](/docs/learners/manage-learners).

## Notifications

On the **Notifications** tab, a table lists activities with an **App** and an **Email** switch for each, so the learner can choose where each kind of notification arrives:

- Space post created
- Space post liked
- Space comment created
- Space comment liked
- Space reply created
- Space reply liked
- Community membership granted

**App** notifications appear under the bell icon in the dashboard header.

## Signing out

The **Log out** button ends the learner's session on that device. It is available at the bottom of the **Profile** tab and in the sidebar menu.

## What learners cannot change

Learners cannot change their email address, and cannot delete their own account from the dashboard. If a learner asks you to remove their data, see [Delete a learner](/docs/learners/delete-learner).
