You can delete a learner, for example when they ask you to remove their personal data. Deleting a learner is permanent.

To suspend a learner without deleting anything, turn off **Portal access** instead. See [Contacts and learners](/docs/learners/manage-learners).

## Before you start

- You need the `contacts:delete` permission.
- You cannot delete the owner of the school. Transfer ownership first.
- If the learner has running subscriptions, CourseLit cancels them with your payment provider first. If the provider cannot be reached, nothing is deleted and you can try again later.

## Remove a contact

<Steps>

<Step>

### Open the contact

Go to **Contacts** and click the learner.

</Step>

<Step>

### Click Remove contact

In the **Danger zone** at the bottom of the page, click **Remove contact**.

</Step>

<Step>

### Confirm

The dialog **Remove this contact?** says that this starts a coordinated removal of the contact from the school, including their profile, learning records, tags and subscription settings, and that it cannot be undone. Click **Remove contact** to continue.

</Step>

</Steps>

You return to the **Contacts** list.

## What happens

Removal happens in two stages.

**Immediately**

- The learner is signed out everywhere and cannot sign in again.
- Their subscriptions, for products and for the community, are cancelled with the payment provider.
- They no longer receive your emails.

**A little later, in the background**

- The contact is deleted from your mailing list.
- Their school account is deleted, together with the records that belong to it: memberships, lesson progress, certificates, downloads and notifications.

Posts and comments the learner wrote in your community are not tied to a person any more once the account is gone.

<Callout type="warning" title="This cannot be undone">

There is no way to restore a removed learner. If they come back, they are a new learner with no history and must be given access again.

</Callout>
