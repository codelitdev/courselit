The **Onboarding** page lists the tasks that make your school ready to sell and to send email. It is available to the school owner, and you open it from **Onboarding** in the admin sidebar. Each task ticks itself off when the work is actually done, so you can do them in any order.

The page shows a progress bar such as "3 of 10 done" and groups tasks into three sections. Each task has a button that takes you to the right screen.

## Get ready to sell

| Task | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Connect payments | Opens **Settings** on the payment tab, where you connect your payment provider. Then you can add a price to your community, or to any course or download. |
| Add your mailing address | Opens the marketing settings. Anti-spam law requires it before you can send any email. |
| Review and publish your Terms of use and Privacy policy | Opens the page editor on the first of the two that is still a draft. They start as drafts with basic text. Your footer and checkout link to them once they are published. |

## Make it yours

| Task | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Edit your homepage | Opens the page editor. Add sections, testimonials or an about section. |
| Connect your own domain | Opens the website settings, where you can use an address like `learn.yourschool.com`. |
| Connect your AI assistant | Opens **Settings** on the AI assistants tab. See [Developers](/docs/developers). |

## Bring people in

| Task | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Invite your first members | Opens **Contacts**. Share your join link, or import the contacts you already have. |
| Set up a welcome email | Opens the sequences area, to greet every new member automatically. |
| Create a course | Opens **New product**. See [Create a product](/docs/products/create-a-product). |
| Invite a team member | Opens **Settings** on the team tab, to share the work with a co-teacher or assistant. |

For more on each area, see [Settings](/docs/settings), [Website](/docs/website), [Marketing](/docs/marketing) and [Learners](/docs/learners).

## Hide the checklist

When every task is done, select **Remove from sidebar**. At any time you can open the **More options** menu on the Onboarding page and choose **Hide from sidebar**. To bring it back, open the **More options** menu on Overview and choose **Show Onboarding in sidebar**.
