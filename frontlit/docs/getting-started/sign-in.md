You sign in to the CourseLit admin dashboard with your email address and a six-digit code. There is no password.

## Sign in with an email code

<Steps>

<Step>

### Enter your email

Open the sign-in page, type your email address in **Email** and select **Send sign-in code**. A short bot check runs when you submit the form.

</Step>

<Step>

### Enter the code

Check your inbox for the code, type it in **Sign-in code** and select **Sign in**. If the code is wrong or has expired, you see "That code is invalid or expired." Request a new one and try again.

</Step>

</Steps>

After you sign in, you land on the Overview page of your active school. If your account has no school yet, CourseLit starts the setup wizard so you can [create your school](/docs/getting-started/create-a-school).

## Sign in as a team member

If a school owner invites you to their team, open the invitation link from your email. Once you accept, you sign in the same way and land in that school. Team members do not see the setup wizard for schools they were invited to.

## Learners sign in somewhere else

Learners do not use the admin dashboard. They sign in on your school's website, also with an email address and a code, usually while signing up for a course or checking out. See [Quickstart](/docs/getting-started/quickstart) for what that looks like.
