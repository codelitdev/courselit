This quickstart takes you from a new school to a published free course that a learner can open. It assumes you already [created your school](/docs/getting-started/create-a-school) and are signed in.

If you chose a free course in the setup wizard, it already exists. In that case, skip ahead to the step that gives a learner access.

<Steps>

<Step>

### Create the course

In the sidebar, open **Products** and select **New product**. Choose **Course**, enter a **Title** such as "Watercolour basics", and select **Create draft**.

The course starts as a draft, so nobody can see it yet. You land on its **Content** tab. For details on this screen, see [Create a product](/docs/products/create-a-product).

</Step>

<Step>

### Add a section and a lesson

On the **Content** tab, select **Add section** and name it. Then select **Add lesson** inside the section, enter a **Title**, write something in the **Content** editor and select **Save and make live**.

Lessons start as drafts by default, and learners only see live lessons. See [Sections and lessons](/docs/products/sections-and-lessons) and [Lesson types](/docs/products/lesson-types).

</Step>

<Step>

### Add a free payment plan

Open the **Settings** tab, find **Payment plans** and select **Add a plan**. Name it, set **Payment type** to **Free** and save.

A course needs at least one payment plan to go live. See [Payment plans](/docs/products/payment-plans).

</Step>

<Step>

### Make the course live

Return to the **Overview** tab. When **Ready to go live?** shows a plan and at least one live lesson, select **Make course live**. A confirmation shows the link to your course's sales page, which you can copy.

By default a new product is **Only people with the link**, so it is not listed on your site's products page. To list it, change **Who can find it** to **Anyone** in **Settings**. See [Publish a product and control visibility](/docs/products/publish-and-visibility).

</Step>

<Step>

### Give a learner access

From the **More actions** menu (the three dots) at the top of the product, select **Invite a customer**. Enter the learner's **Name** and **Email** and select **Invite customer**.

CourseLit creates a learner membership for that email address and sends the learner an email with the subject "You have been invited to" followed by your course title. Inviting only works once the product is live.

</Step>

<Step>

### See what the learner sees

Open the invitation email in a private browser window, as the learner would. The learner:

1. Selects **Sign in to get started** in the email, enters their email address and then the code CourseLit sends them.
2. Lands in the course viewer, with your sections and lessons in a sidebar.
3. Reads each lesson and selects **Mark complete** when they finish it.
4. Finds the course any time under **Products** in their dashboard, with a progress bar.

Learners can also get a free course without an invitation. On the sales page they select **Start learning for free**, sign in with their email and select **Get for free** at checkout.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Preview without a learner account

You can look at the course as a learner would without creating a membership. On the product page, select **Preview**. See [Preview a course](/docs/products/preview-a-course).
