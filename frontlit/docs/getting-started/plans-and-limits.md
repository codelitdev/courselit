CourseLit Cloud has two paid plans, **Pro** and **Business**, and every new school starts with a free trial. Self-hosted CourseLit has every feature and no limits. See [Cloud or self-hosted](/docs/getting-started/cloud-vs-self-hosted).

None of the plans charge a fee on your sales. Learners pay through your own payment provider account, and only that provider's fees apply. See [Set up payments](/docs/settings/payments).

## Plans

| | Pro | Business |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price | $29 a month or $290 a year | $79 a month or $790 a year |
| Courses, downloads and learners | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Team members | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Community, website, blog and custom domain | Included | Included |
| Email marketing (broadcasts, sequences, drip emails) | 10,000 emails a month | 50,000 emails a month |
| Storage | 20 GB | 200 GB |
| Remove CourseLit branding | Yes | Yes |
| Code injection | Yes | Yes |
| REST API, API keys and MCP server | Yes | Yes |
| SCORM lessons | No | Yes |
| Learner single sign-on (SAML) | No | Yes |
| Audit log | No | Yes |
| Support | Email | Priority |

Paying yearly costs the same as ten months.

The email limit counts marketing email only. Sign-in codes, receipts, access emails and other messages your learners need always go out and never count towards it.

## Free trial

Every account gets one 14-day free trial, with no card needed.

- The trial includes every Business feature, except removing CourseLit branding.
- Code you add with code injection runs only for you, the school owner, not for visitors.
- Marketing emails don't send during the trial. Learners still get sign-in codes, receipts and access emails.
- You have 500 MB of storage.
- A custom domain works, but emails CourseLit sends to learners link to your school's courselit.app address until you choose a plan.

To keep your school, choose a plan from [Billing and plan](/docs/settings/billing-and-plan) before the trial ends.

## When a trial or plan ends

If a trial ends without a plan, or a subscription ends, your school becomes read-only for 15 days. You can still sign in and choose a plan in that time. After 15 days, the school and all its data are deleted, including its files and contacts.

<Callout type="warning" title="Deletion can't be undone">

Choose a plan within the 15 days if you want to keep your school.

</Callout>
