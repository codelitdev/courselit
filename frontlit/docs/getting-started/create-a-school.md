The setup wizard creates your school and walks you through your first offer, your site's look and your email setup. It starts automatically the first time you sign in without a school. You can also start it later by choosing **Add school** in the school switcher, or select **New school** on the Schools page.

Everything after naming your school is optional, and you can change any choice later in the normal admin screens.

## Before you begin

- Sign in to the admin dashboard. See [Sign in](/docs/getting-started/sign-in).
- Decide what you want to offer first: a course, a community or a download. You can add the others later.

## Walk through the wizard

<Steps>

<Step>

### Tell us your name

On your first run, the wizard asks "First, what should we call you?" Enter **Your name**, the name your learners know you by. It appears on your emails, your community posts and the certificates you issue. This step is skipped when you create a second school.

</Step>

<Step>

### Name your school

Fill in:

- **School name**, for example "Inkwell Studio".
- **Site address**, which is suggested from your name and checked for availability. It must be 3 to 63 characters, using lowercase letters, numbers and single hyphens. If it is taken, the wizard suggests alternatives.
- **Currency**, which sets the prices learners see. The choices are USD, EUR, GBP, INR, AUD and CAD.
- **Language**, for your site and learner emails. The choices are English, Español, Français, Deutsch, हिन्दी and Português.

When you continue, CourseLit creates your school. It sets up your website, with a homepage and drafts of your Terms and Privacy pages, your community (ready for when you switch it on) and your email setup. If a part fails, you can retry it.

</Step>

<Step>

### Choose your first offer

Pick **A community**, **A course** or **A download**, or select **I'll decide later** to skip ahead.

</Step>

<Step>

### Set up the offer

The form depends on your choice.

| Offer | What you enter |
| --- | --- |
| A course | **Course title**, **Short description**, an optional **Cover image**, a **First lesson** (a title and either text or a video) and a **Price**. |
| A community | **Community name**, **Description**, **Who can join** (**Anyone, instantly** or **By request**, with a **Question for applicants**), a **Price** and a **Welcome message** for new members. |
| A download | **Title**, **Description**, **Files** and a **Price**. A free download works as a lead magnet. |

Choose **Free** to publish the offer when your school goes live. If you choose **Paid**, the wizard does not take the price: it stays hidden from your site until you connect payments and add a payment plan from your dashboard.

If you prefer to build in a chat with an AI assistant, this step also offers a way to connect one. See [Set up your first offer with an AI assistant](/docs/setup).

</Step>

<Step>

### Make your site yours

Add an optional **Logo**, pick a **Theme**, and write a **Tagline** shown under your school's name on the homepage. You can fine-tune colours and fonts later in the page editor.

</Step>

<Step>

### Get ready to send email

Anti-spam law requires a postal address in every marketing email, so the wizard asks for your **Street address**, **City**, **State or region** (optional), **Postal code** and **Country**. The address appears in the footer of the emails you send. Set a **Sender name**, which is who your emails say they are from.

You can choose **Skip for now**. Email then stays off until you add a mailing address.

</Step>

<Step>

### Choose a plan (hosted CourseLit only)

On hosted CourseLit, the wizard asks you to pick a plan and billing period, then continues to checkout. If you cancel the checkout, you return to the wizard with your choices kept. Self-hosted installations do not show this step.

</Step>

<Step>

### Go live

The last screen confirms your school is live. It shows your site address with **Open site**, a link to share your first offer, and a **Waiting on your dashboard** list of anything you still need to do, such as connecting payments for a paid offer.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Leave and come back

The wizard saves your answers as you go. Select **Finish later** at any time. The next time you sign in, it resumes where you stopped. Use **Back** to revisit a step and **Skip for now** to skip the optional ones.

## What to do next

Your remaining setup tasks appear in the Onboarding checklist. See [Finish setting up your school](/docs/getting-started/setup-checklist), or follow the [Quickstart](/docs/getting-started/quickstart) to publish a course.
