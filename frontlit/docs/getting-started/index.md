CourseLit is a platform for teachers, coaches and creators who want to sell or share what they know from their own website. You create a **school**, add **courses**, **digital downloads** and a **community**, and learners sign up and learn on your school's public site.

You run everything from the admin dashboard. Your learners never see it: they use your school's website to browse, sign up, learn and join your community.

## What you can do with CourseLit

- Teach with **courses** made of sections and lessons: text, video, audio, PDF, files, embeds, quizzes and SCORM packages.
- Sell or give away **digital downloads**, including free ones that grow your mailing list.
- Run a **community** with spaces where members post, comment and react.
- Charge with one-time, subscription or instalment **payment plans**, or offer things for free.
- Build your public **website** with a page editor, and email your contacts.
- Work with a **team** and share the load with co-teachers or assistants.
