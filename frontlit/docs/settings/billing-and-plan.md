On hosted CourseLit, your school runs on a CourseLit plan that is billed to you, the owner. This is separate from the payments your learners make to you, which are covered in [Payments](/docs/settings/payments).

Only the school owner can see and manage the plan. Open it from your name at the bottom of the sidebar, then **Billing and plan**. The menu item appears only for owners of schools on hosted CourseLit. Self-hosted schools have no CourseLit plan, and the page says so.

## Plans

CourseLit offers a **Pro** plan and a **Business** plan, billed monthly or yearly. You pick one when you set up your school, on the **Pick a plan to go live** step. You pay on the CourseLit checkout page and then come back to the setup screen as your school goes live. See [Create your school](/docs/getting-started/create-a-school) in Getting started.

If you haven't chosen a plan yet, the page says "This school has no paid plan yet" and shows a **Choose a plan** button. If a checkout is in progress, it tells you the plan will show here once the payment completes.

## Read your plan

The page shows the plan name, a status badge and what you pay, for example the amount per month or per year.

| Status badge | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Active | The plan is paid and running. |
| Free trial | You are in a free trial. The page shows the date it ends. |
| Ends soon | You cancelled. The plan stays active until the end of the paid period. |
| Payment due | The last payment couldn't be collected. Update your payment method with your CourseLit billing contact. |
| Cancelled | The plan has ended. |

Below the badge, the page shows when the plan renews, or when it will end.

## Cancel your plan

<Steps>

<Step>

### Click Cancel plan

On the **Billing and plan** page, click **Cancel plan**.

</Step>

<Step>

### Confirm

The dialog says your plan stays active until the end of the paid period and that you won't be charged again. Click **Cancel plan** to confirm, or **Keep my plan** to go back.

</Step>

</Steps>

You see "Your plan will end at the close of the paid period." and the badge changes to **Ends soon**.

## Resume your plan

Until the paid period ends, click **Resume plan** to keep it renewing. You see "Your plan will keep renewing."

<Callout type="note">

Billing actions need your own signed-in browser session. They are not available to API keys or AI assistants.

</Callout>
