On hosted CourseLit, your school runs on a CourseLit plan that is billed to you, the owner. This is separate from the payments your learners make to you, which are covered in [Payments](/docs/settings/payments).

Only the school owner can see and manage the plan. Open it from your name at the bottom of the sidebar, then **Billing and plan**. The menu item appears only for owners of schools on hosted CourseLit. Self-hosted schools have no CourseLit plan, and the page says so.

## Plans

CourseLit offers a **Pro** plan and a **Business** plan, billed monthly or yearly.

| | Pro | Business |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price | $29 a month or $290 a year | $79 a month or $790 a year |
| Marketing email | 25,000 a month | 100,000 a month |
| Storage | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Remove CourseLit branding and add code to your site | Yes | Yes |
| SCORM lessons, learner SSO, the audit log and priority support | No | Yes |

There is no limit on the number of team members.

## Free trial

The first school on your account gets a free 14-day trial. It needs no card, and each account gets one trial. The trial has every Business feature except removing CourseLit branding and running injected code for everyone. It does not include marketing email, and it has 500 MB of storage.

Any school you add later must pick a plan during setup, on the **Pick a plan to go live** step. You pay on the CourseLit checkout page and then come back to the setup screen as your school goes live. Until you pay, the site stays offline and visitors see "This site isn't available." See [Create your school](/docs/getting-started/create-a-school).

During a trial, the page shows "Business plan" with the badge **Free trial**, the text "A free trial with every Business feature. Pick Pro or Business before it ends to keep your school running.", a **Choose a plan** button and "Your free trial ends on" followed by the date.

If you haven't chosen a plan yet, the page says "This school has no paid plan yet" and shows a **Choose a plan** button. If a checkout is in progress, it tells you the plan will show here once the payment completes.

## Usage

The page shows two meters.

- **Storage** shows what you use against your limit, for example "425 MB of 500 MB".
- **Marketing email this month** shows, for example, "21,000 of 25,000". During a trial it says "Not included" with the note "Sending marketing email starts with a paid plan. Sign-in codes and receipts go out during your trial."

At 80% of a limit, you see a banner such as "Inkwell Studio has used 85% of its storage." with a **See your plan** link, and you get one email.

- For storage, which is limited only during a trial, you get an email each time usage crosses 80%. You get another only after usage drops below 70% and crosses 80% again.
- For marketing email, you get one email a calendar month, on paid plans only.

If you go over a limit, nothing is deleted.

| Limit | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Storage (trial only) | New uploads are refused. |
| Marketing email | Broadcasts, sequences and drip emails wait until next month. Sign-in codes and receipts still go out. |

## When a trial or plan ends

When a trial or a plan ends, the school becomes read-only for 15 days. Learners keep their access and can sign in, but nobody can buy, join or sign up, and your team can't change anything. As the owner, you can still choose a plan. You get an email titled "Inkwell Studio is now read-only". After the 15 days, the school is deleted.

The admin shows a banner with "Inkwell Studio is read-only. Learners keep their access, but nothing can change until you pick a plan." and a **Choose a plan** link. Other team members see "Inkwell Studio is read-only until its owner picks a plan." A school that is waiting for checkout shows "Inkwell Studio isn't live yet. Pick a plan to open it to learners."

## Read your plan

The page shows the plan name, a status badge and what you pay, for example the amount per month or per year.

| Status badge | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Active | The plan is paid and running. |
| Free trial | You are in a free trial. The page shows the date it ends. |
| Ends soon | You cancelled. The plan stays active until the end of the paid period. |
| Payment due | The last payment couldn't be collected. Update your payment method with your CourseLit billing contact. |
| Cancelled | The plan has ended. |

Below the badge, the page shows when the plan renews, or when it will end.

## Cancel your plan

<Steps>

<Step>

### Click Cancel plan

On the **Billing and plan** page, click **Cancel plan**.

</Step>

<Step>

### Confirm

The dialog says your plan stays active until the end of the paid period and that you won't be charged again. Click **Cancel plan** to confirm, or **Keep my plan** to go back.

</Step>

</Steps>

You see "Your plan will end at the close of the paid period." and the badge changes to **Ends soon**.

## Resume your plan

Until the paid period ends, click **Resume plan** to keep it renewing. You see "Your plan will keep renewing."

<Callout type="note">

Billing actions need your own signed-in browser session. They are not available to API keys or AI assistants.

</Callout>
