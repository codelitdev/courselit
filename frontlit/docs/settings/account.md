Your account is you, the person who signs in to the admin dashboard. It is separate from your school. The same account can belong to several schools.

## Open your account

Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar and choose **Account**.

## Change your name and photo

The **Account** page has two cards.

- **Display picture** shows your photo, visible to your teammates. Click **Change photo** to upload a JPG, PNG or WebP image of up to 2 MB, or **Remove** to clear it.
- **Details** shows your **Email** and your **Name**. Edit the name to change how you appear to your teammates.

Click **Save changes** to apply them. The button is enabled only when something has changed, and **Profile updated successfully.** confirms it. Your name is required.

Your email address can't be changed here. It is managed through authentication.

## Log out

Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar and choose **Log out**. You return to the sign-in page.

## Billing

If you own the school on hosted CourseLit, the same menu has a **Billing and plan** entry. See [Billing and plan](/docs/settings/billing-and-plan).
