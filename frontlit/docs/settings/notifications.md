Notifications tell you when something happens in your school, such as a sale or a new community post. You choose, for each kind of activity, whether you hear about it in the app, by email, both or neither.

These settings apply only to you. Other team members set their own.

## Change your notifications

<Steps>

<Step>

### Open Notifications

Go to **Settings** then **Notifications**.

</Step>

<Step>

### Flip the switches

The table has a row for each kind of activity, with an **In app** switch and an **Email** switch. A change is saved as soon as you flip a switch. If it can't be saved, the switch goes back and an error message appears.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Kinds of activity

| Activity | When it applies |
| --- | --- |
| New community posts | Someone posts in a community. |
| New comments | Someone comments in a community. |
| New replies | Someone replies to a comment. |
| Community membership requests | Someone asks to join a community that needs approval. |
| New community members | Someone joins a community. |
| New sales | A learner buys something from your school. |
| Checkouts your payment provider refused | Your payment provider refused to start a learner's checkout. This notice goes to the owner and to team members with `commerce:manage`, at most once an hour, and points you to your payment settings. |

## Read your notifications

The bell at the top of the dashboard opens your in-app notifications. When there is nothing new, it says **No new notifications**.
