The audit log shows "Who changed what in your school, newest first." Open **Settings** then **Audit log**. You need the `school:write` permission.

The audit log comes with the Business plan, and with a free trial. On other plans you see "The audit log comes with the Business plan." See [Billing and plan](/docs/settings/billing-and-plan).

## Read the log

The table has four columns.

| Column | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| When | The date and time of the change. |
| Who | The person who made it. |
| What | What they did. |
| Resource | The thing they changed. |

Select **Show older** to load more entries.

<Callout type="note">

Billing events are visible only to the school owner.

</Callout>
