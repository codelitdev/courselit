---
title: Switch or add a school
description: Move between the schools you can access and create another school from the admin dashboard.

---

You can belong to more than one school, for example one you own and one you help run as a team member. The admin dashboard always works on one school at a time, called the active school. Everything you see, from products to contacts and settings, belongs to it.

## Switch schools

<Steps>

<Step>

### Open the school menu

At the top of the sidebar, click the box showing the current school's name and address.

</Step>

<Step>

### Choose a school

The **Schools** list shows every school you can access, with its address and a check mark on the active one. Click another school. The dashboard reloads on that school.

</Step>

</Steps>

You can also open the **Schools** page at `/schools`. It shows a card per school with its address, its status and an **Active** label. Click **Select** on a school to switch to it, or use the link icon to open its public site.

## Add a school

Click **Add school** at the bottom of the school menu, or **New school** on the Schools page. The setup wizard opens for a new school, where you name it, choose a site address, currency and language, and pick a first offer. See [Create your school](/docs/getting-started/create-a-school).

On hosted CourseLit, each school you create has its own CourseLit plan. See [Billing and plan](/docs/settings/billing-and-plan).

## Join a school as a team member

When someone invites you to their school, you accept from the invitation link and the school then appears in your school list. See [Manage your team](/docs/settings/team).

## What belongs to a school

Your products, learners, website, email and settings are all separate for each school. Changing the active school never copies anything between them. Your own account, including your name and photo, is shared across all your schools. See [Your account](/docs/settings/account).
