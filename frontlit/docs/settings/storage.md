Learners can upload files to your communities and to their profile. Their uploads count toward your school's storage. Open **Settings** then **Storage** to set how much each learner can upload. You need the `school:write` permission.

## Set the limit

Change **Upload limit per learner (MB)**. The default is 100. The help text reads "How much each learner can upload to your communities and their profile. It counts toward your school's storage. Up to 1024 MB." Save your change.

See [Uploading files](/docs/communities/member-experience) for what learners can upload, and [Billing and plan](/docs/settings/billing-and-plan) for your school's storage limit and usage.
