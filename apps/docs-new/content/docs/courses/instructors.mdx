---
title: Instructors
description: Show who teaches your course and each of its sections
---

Instructors are profiles of the people who teach. Add them once, then assign them to a course or to individual sections. Students see them on the course page, next to section titles in the course outline, and under the lesson title.

## Create an instructor

1. Go to **Dashboard → Instructors** and click **New instructor**.
2. Add a name (required), and optionally a photo, a short bio and links such as a website or a social profile. Links must start with `http://` or `https://`.
3. Click **Save**.

An instructor does not need an account on your school.

## Assign instructors to a course

1. Open your course from **Dashboard → Products** and click **Manage**.
2. In the **Instructors** section, add the instructors in the order you want them shown.

## Assign instructors to a section

When a section is taught by someone other than the course instructors, open the section from the course content page and pick its instructors in **Instructors**. The section's instructors replace the course instructors for that section only.

## What students see

- **Course page:** an **Instructors** block with photo, name, bio and links for everyone assigned to the course or to any of its sections.
- **Course outline:** small avatars next to the title of sections that have their own instructors.
- **Lesson page:** "with _name_" below the title. It shows the section's instructors, or the course's when the section has none.

Nothing is shown when no instructor is assigned.

## Delete an instructor

Deleting an instructor removes them from every course and section they were assigned to. Their photo is deleted too.
